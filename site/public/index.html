Google Contacts

Email can sync the contacts of a Gmail or Google Workspace account with its address book. You sign in on Google's own page in your browser and allow Email to see and edit your contacts. Email then reads them so they appear in the address book, and writes back the changes you make there: editing, adding or deleting a contact.

Your contacts go straight between Google and your computer. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Read the privacy policy for details.
