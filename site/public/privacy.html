Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Email is a desktop email app that runs on your computer. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and what happens to the data it gets from your Google account.

The short version

  • There is no Email server. The app talks directly to your mail providers (such as Google, Microsoft or your own IMAP server) from your computer.
  • We, the developers, never receive your mail, your contacts, your passwords or any other personal data.
  • The app has no analytics, tracking or advertising.

What stays on your computer

Your messages, contacts, settings and search index are stored on your computer so the app works quickly and offline. Passwords, sign-in tokens and API keys are kept in your operating system's keychain or credential store. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes all of it.

Google user data

If you choose to connect Google Contacts, Email asks Google for permission to see and edit your contacts (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts scope) and for your email address (openid email), so it knows which account the contacts belong to.

What Email does with it

  • Reads your contacts and contact groups so they appear in Email's address book, for example to complete addresses as you type and to show a sender's name and photo.
  • Writes to Google only when you change a contact in Email: when you edit, add or delete one, the change is made in your Google Contacts too.
  • Stores a copy of the contacts, their photos and Google's sign-in token only on your computer. The token is kept in the system keychain.

What Email does not do with it

  • It does not send your Google data to us or to any server other than Google's.
  • It does not share, sell or transfer your Google data to anyone.
  • It does not use your Google data for advertising, profiling, or training AI or machine-learning models.
  • Google data is not included in what the optional writing help sends to an AI service (see below).
  • No person reads your Google data. The developers have no access to it.

Email's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting

Disconnect in Settings › Accounts, or removing the account, deletes the account's contacts and sign-in token from your computer. It changes nothing in Google. You can also remove Email's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other connections the app makes

  • Your mail providers, to fetch and send your mail and to sign in (Google, Microsoft, or the servers you set up).
  • Senders' domains, to fetch a company's logo (its published mail logo or its website icon). Only the domain name is looked up, never your address or the message.
  • GitHub, to check for and download app updates. You can turn this off in Settings › Troubleshooting › Updates.
  • Anthropic, only if you turn on the optional writing help with your own API key, and only when you ask it for help. What is sent is the message you are replying to, what you have written and your instruction. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to that.

Children

Email is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
