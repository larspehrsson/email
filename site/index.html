Vibe-Coded Email Client

A fast desktop email app for Linux, macOS and Windows. All your accounts in one window, your mail kept on your computer so everything opens instantly, and a keyboard for everything.

Download

What it does

  • Reads, searches, organizes and sends mail for Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook, Microsoft 365 and any other IMAP account.
  • Keeps a copy of your mail on your computer, so it opens and searches instantly, even offline.
  • Optionally syncs your Google Contacts with its address book, both ways.

Signing in with Google

Gmail and Google Workspace accounts can sign in on Google's own page. Email then asks Google for:

  • Your Gmail (https://mail.google.com/): to download, show, search and organize your mail and to send what you write. Google's mail servers (IMAP and SMTP) accept only this permission.
  • Your contacts, only if you connect Google Contacts: to sync them with the address book.
  • Your email address: to know which account signed in.

Everything Email gets from Google stays on your computer. There's no server of ours in between, and nothing is sent to us. The privacy policy has the details.

Open source

Email is free and open source. The code, the builds and a trust report listing every connection it can make are on GitHub.
