Privacy policy

Email is a desktop app that runs on your computer. It has no servers of its own, no accounts with us, and it sends us nothing.

Effective November 26, 2026. Earlier versions are in the history of this page.

What Email collects

Nothing, for us. There are no analytics, crash reports, usage statistics or advertising. The people who make Email never receive your mail, your contacts, your passwords or anything about how you use the app.

What Email keeps on your computer

  • Your mail: a copy of your messages and folders, so they open quickly and can be searched offline.
  • Your address book, including contacts synced from Google if you connect Google Contacts.
  • Your passwords and sign-ins (including the tokens Google and Microsoft give it), kept in your system's keychain.
  • Your settings.

This data stays on your computer. Removing an account in Email deletes its mail, its synced Google contacts and its sign-in from your computer. Nothing is deleted from your mail provider.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Email asks for access to:

  • Gmail (https://mail.google.com/): to download your messages and folders, show and search them, organize them (read, star, label, archive, move, delete) when you ask, and send the messages you write. Google's IMAP and SMTP mail servers accept only this permission.
  • Google Contacts, only if you connect them: to show your contacts in the address book and to save the changes you make there back to Google.
  • Your email address (OpenID): to confirm which account signed in.

Email uses this data only to provide those features to you, on your computer. It doesn't send Google user data to us or to anyone else, except as described under "Services you turn on" below, and only when you ask. It doesn't sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it.

Email's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Email's access at any time in your Google account's Third-party apps & services.

Who Email talks to

  • Your mail providers (Google, Microsoft, or the servers you set up), to sync and send your mail and, if you connect them, your contacts.
  • GitHub, to check for and download updates. It can be turned off in Settings.

Services you turn on

These are off until you turn them on, and only do what you ask:

  • Writing help and questions about a message: the text you ask about, or the message you're asking about, goes to Anthropic, under your own Anthropic API key and your own agreement with Anthropic.
  • AI apps: AI apps on your own computer (such as Claude Desktop) can read and organize your mail through Email, for the accounts you choose. Deleting and sending ask you every time. What such an app does with what it reads is governed by that app.
  • Sender pictures: Email looks up a picture or logo from the sender's own website. Only the domain is used; no mail content is sent.

Children

Email isn't directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

Changes and questions

Changes to this policy are published on this page, with the history linked above. Questions: open an issue at github.com/rickhall/email/issues.
